DHA Margalla Enclave: Signature Foothills Living & Prime Commercial Landmark

Positioned against the iconic, scenic canvas of the Margalla Hills corridor with direct arterial connection to Margalla Avenue and Ring Road, DHA Margalla Enclave represents the gold standard of planned urban development in the Islamabad Capital Territory.

Featuring Ballot 1 (Residential Plots: 125, 250, and 500 Sq. Yds.), Ballot 2 (Commercial Plots: 100, 133.25, 200, and 500 Sq. Yds.), and Ballot 3 (Approved LG+G+5 High-Rise Commercial: 133.33, 200, and 266.66 Sq. Yds.), this premier enclave offers unmatched capital security, computerized transparent balloting, and structured 1-year, 2-year, and 3-year quarterly payment milestones with a 20% down payment or discounted lump-sum settlement.

  • Prestigious DHA Masterplanning: Wide 120-ft and 80-ft dual boulevards, underground electrification, dedicated grid stations, and modern stormwater drainage networks.
  • Approved LG+G+5 Multi-Storey Commercials: Dedicated commercial zones permitting Lower Ground + Ground + 5 storeys for retail malls, executive offices, and luxury suites.
  • High Capital Growth Boulevard Commercials: 100 Sq Yds to 500 Sq Yds prime commercial plots engineered for high-street retail, corporate arcades, and international brand plazas.
  • Lump Sum & Milestone Installment Options: Pay upfront within 30 days of ballot or choose 4, 8, or 12 easy quarterly installments with a 20% down payment.
