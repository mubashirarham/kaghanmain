Legal Buyer's Guide

Official DHA Islamabad Allotment & Transfer Procedure

Step-by-step guidance on how plots in DHA Margalla Enclave are transferred with full legal security under Kaghan Properties supervision.

1

File Verification

Verification of computerized ballot allotment / intimation letter at DHA Phase 1 Head Office.

2

NDC Clearance

Seller obtains No Demand Certificate (NDC) confirming 100% dues and installments are up to date.

3

Tax Challans

Deposit of applicable FBR withholding taxes (Section 236K / 236C) and DHA transfer charges.

4

Biometric Transfer

Buyer & Seller appear before the DHA Transfer Officer for biometric verification and paper execution.

5

Allotment Letter

Official original DHA Allotment Letter is handed over to the new owner within 5 to 7 working days.
