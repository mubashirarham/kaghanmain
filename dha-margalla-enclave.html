DHA Margalla Enclave FAQs

Key answers regarding balloting, transfers, down payments, and location.

Where is DHA Margalla Enclave located in Islamabad?

DHA Margalla Enclave is located in CDA Zone 4 along Main Jinnah Avenue Islamabad, right opposite Serena Hotel / Club Road access corridor, adjacent to Bahria Enclave and Park Road.

What is the down payment required for 3rd Ballot plots?

3rd Ballot Residential and Commercial plots require a 20% Down Payment payable within 30 days of the ballot date. The remaining balance is payable in 1, 2, or 3-Year quarterly installments.

Can Overseas Pakistanis book plots without visiting Pakistan?

Yes! Kaghan Properties provides dedicated end-to-end overseas concierge services. Overseas buyers can transfer funds via official banking channels (Roshan Digital Account or direct swift transfer) and receive scanned & courier-delivered computerized allotment files.

What are the discounts for upfront Lump Sum payment?

Investors paying 100% upfront within 30 days receive significant price concessions. For example, a 5 Marla 3rd Ballot plot on the 3-Year plan is PKR 25,000,000, whereas the Lump Sum price is only PKR 21,434,375.
