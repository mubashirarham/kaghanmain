Investor Guide • Islamabad Zone 4

Investing in DHA Margalla Enclave Islamabad

DHA Margalla Enclave Islamabad is one of the capital's most high-profile planned communities, developed as an official joint venture between the Capital Development Authority (CDA) and Defence Housing Authority (DHA) Islamabad-Rawalpindi. Positioned in Zone 4 near the scenic Margalla Foothills and Botanical Garden, the enclave offers direct access via Main Jinnah Avenue, Kuri Road, and Park Road — just minutes from Bahria Enclave, Park View City, and Zero Point.

For both end-users and capital-growth investors, DHA Margalla Enclave stands out because of its clear government-backed master plan, wide 40ft to 120ft avenues, dedicated Lake District, Grand Mosque, and flexible 1-Year, 2-Year, and 3-Year quarterly installment plans with a 20% down payment. Buyers looking for immediate savings can also opt for discounted lump-sum pricing across Ballot 1 (5 Marla, 10 Marla, 1 Kanal residential), Ballot 2 (Main Boulevard commercial), and Ballot 3 (LG+G+5 high-rise commercial plazas).

Is DHA Margalla Enclave approved by CDA?
Yes. DHA Margalla Enclave is an official Joint Venture between CDA and DHA Islamabad-Rawalpindi in Zone 4, offering complete legal security and transparent transfer procedures.
What is the down payment for Ballot 1, 2 & 3 plots?
Standard installment plans require a 20% down payment, with the balance spread across 1, 2, or 3 years in quarterly installments. Lump-sum buyers receive an official upfront discount.
