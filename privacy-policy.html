Section 2

Information We Collect

To facilitate property transactions, verify client requirements, and provide accurate real estate intelligence, we may collect the following categories of information:

A. Information You Directly Provide

  • Contact Credentials: Full name, email address, direct phone number, and WhatsApp contact handle.
  • Property Investment Criteria: Preferred asset type (residential villa, residential plot, commercial shop, alpine chalet), budget range (in PKR, USD, or AED), and target location (e.g., Bahria Enclave, DHA Margalla Enclave, CDA Sectors).
  • Viewing & Booking Requests: Scheduled appointment dates, preferred inspection times, and questions submitted via inquiry forms.
  • Identity & Legal Documentation (Transaction Stage Only): National Identity Card (CNIC), National Identity Card for Overseas Pakistanis (NICOP), passport copies, and Special Power of Attorney (SPA) records strictly required when executing legal property transfers or allotment paperwork.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Telemetry Data: IP address, browser type and version, device operating system, language selection, and screen dimensions.
  • Usage Metrics: Pages visited, listings browsed, time spent per listing, search query filters executed, and referring URLs.
  • Preference State: Selected display currency (PKR, USD, AED) and chosen area unit (Marla, Kanal, Sq. Ft.) saved in your local browser storage.

Section 4

Legal Grounds for Processing

Under Pakistan’s Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act (PECA 2016), federal property statutes, and international privacy benchmarks, we process your information on the following legal bases:

  • Explicit Consent: Provided whenever you voluntarily complete an inquiry form, request a callback, or message us on WhatsApp.
  • Contractual Performance: Necessary to draft, verify, and complete sale agreements, token receipts, or construction contracts requested by you.
  • Compliance with Legal Mandates: Adhering to property verification requirements governed by CDA, RDA, DHA, FBR tax withholding regulations (Sections 7E and 236K), and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) / Know Your Customer (KYC) standards.
  • Legitimate Interests: Protecting the integrity of our platform against fraudulent listings, bot spam, and denial-of-service disruptions.
Section 5

Zero Data-Sale Guarantee & Third Parties

Our Ironclad Commitment: We Never Sell Your Data

Kaghan Properties never sells, rents, monetizes, or leases your personal contact details, investment preferences, or financial inquiries to any third-party marketing networks, lead aggregators, or unauthorized telemarketers.

Information is shared strictly on a confidential need-to-know basis with trusted technology partners who support our operations:

Infrastructure & Cloud Database Providers: Google Cloud Firebase (Firestore secure databases in compliant data regions) and Netlify Edge Network for site delivery.
Media Delivery Networks: Cloudinary for verified property photography and architectural floor plan storage.

Section 6

Overseas Expatriate Data Handling

A significant portion of our clientele comprises overseas Pakistanis residing in the United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, the United Kingdom, the European Union, the United States, and Canada.

When dealing with cross-border real estate investments, we apply heightened confidentiality standards:

  • Special Power of Attorney (SPA) Protection: Embassies and consulates require sensitive identity credentials for legal attestation. These documents are stored within encrypted partitions accessible only by senior legal directors.
  • Roshan Digital Account (RDA) Compliance: We provide formal payment verification certificates strictly through verified banking channels without transmitting confidential bank logins or financial tokens.
  • Cross-Border Transfer Protections: Any international data transfer strictly complies with internationally recognized data protection principles to guarantee your information receives equivalent protections as afforded in your home jurisdiction.

Section 8

Data Security & Encryption

We implement rigorous organizational, technical, and architectural safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized disclosure, loss, or manipulation:

  • TLS 1.3 Transport Encryption: All digital communications and form submissions are transmitted via verified SSL/TLS encrypted sessions.
  • Firestore Security Rules: Role-based access control (RBAC) ensures only authenticated agents and verified administrators can view internal client records.
  • Edge Rate Limiting: Incoming API requests are monitored at the CDN boundary to prevent automated screen scraping and lead harvest exploitation.
  • Strict Environmental Secret Isolation: SMTP passwords, database keys, and backend API credentials reside strictly in serverless environment variables, never exposed in client-facing source code.
Section 9

Retention Period & Your Legal Rights

We retain your inquiry data only for as long as necessary to provide our advisory services, resolve customer queries, or fulfill statutory record-keeping periods mandated by tax and land revenue authorities.

Your Entitled Rights:

Right of Access: You may request a copy of the personal information we hold regarding your inquiries.
Right of Rectification: You may update or correct outdated contact numbers, email addresses, or budget requirements.
Right of Erasure: You have the right to request deletion of your lead record from our CRM databases at any time.
Right to Opt-Out: You can opt out of property alert updates or WhatsApp advisory follow-ups with a single reply.
